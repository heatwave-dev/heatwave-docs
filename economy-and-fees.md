# Understand credits, costs and withdrawals

Reviewed October 23, 2026 · [Documentation home](README.md) · [Live earnings guide](https://heatwave.gg/codex-en.html#gains)

Recorded activity, confirmed game credit and external wallet HTW are separate amounts. Read the status beside each figure. Your current review supplies exact prices, eligibility, fees and limits before approval.

## How work becomes credit

The server records eligible work under each trip’s saved departure terms. Current rates do not reprice earlier trips. Up to eight hours of eligible away progress use half the current open-game rate. Waiting, rest and unrecorded gaps do not create guaranteed productive time.

Assessment runs after the relevant UTC day closes. It can include eligible work in an open mission without completing it. There is no guaranteed midnight credit.

Funding depends on backing and the daily budget. The revenue-linked ceiling is 70% of average observed reserve inflows over up to seven settled daily intervals. Demand above the available budget is allocated proportionally. Only the funded amount becomes credit. A partial or zero allocation creates no debt or promise to make up the difference. A later loss does not erase confirmed credit.

The October 15 description of a blanket 3%-of-free-reserve daily issuance budget is not the current player rule. The 3% withdrawal fee is a different charge. Hourly, 24-hour and fiat references are estimates, not guaranteed credit or income.

## Reference costs

These reference costs do not replace your current review:

| Action | HTW cost | SOL treatment |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Basic box | 25,000 | Quoted fees and any account deposit |
| Five boxes | 125,000 | Quoted fees and any account deposit |
| Premium box | 1,000,000 | Quoted fees and any account deposit |
| Common → Uncommon fusion | 50,000 credited HTW and 8 eligible Common cars | 0.005 SOL plus applicable network costs |
| Uncommon → Rare fusion | 100,000 credited HTW and 4 eligible Uncommon cars | 0.005 SOL plus applicable network costs |
| Rare → Epic fusion | 300,000 credited HTW and 5 eligible Rare cars | 0.005 SOL plus applicable network costs |
| Epic → Legendary fusion | 750,000 credited HTW and 3 eligible Epic cars | 0.005 SOL plus applicable network costs |
| Auto-Farm Pass | 50,000 credited HTW for seven days | 0.005 SOL plus applicable network costs |

Fusion permanently consumes input cars and draws a random model of the next supported rarity. Half its HTW is burned in the game and half returns to the reserve. The pass burns 20% and returns 80%. Neither action guarantees income or withdrawal eligibility.

Garages and the mechanic are optional; outside recovery is free. The review quotes facility and other optional purchase costs. A completed player-market sale pays confirmed net proceeds to the external wallet, not Earnings. A listing or reservation is not a completed sale.

## Withdrawal requirements and fees

Before the first successful withdrawal, wait seven days from confirmed on-chain registration. Login and holding do not start that clock. It applies once and does not restart after every successful withdrawal.

The fixed requirements are:

| Requirement | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Minimum gross credited HTW | 100,000 HTW |
| Holding in the same external wallet before payout | 2,500 HTW |
| Token fee | 3% of gross HTW |
| Net token share | 97% of gross HTW |
| SOL game fee | 0.0025 SOL |
| Other SOL costs | Quoted network fee and any required account rent |

The 2,500 HTW holding check is not another token charge. Your wallet also needs the quoted SOL costs.

For example, a gross request of 100,000 HTW has a 3,000 HTW token fee and a 97,000 HTW net amount. SOL costs remain separate. This arithmetic does not prove account eligibility or a completed payment.

## Rolling limits and saved requests

New requests have per-request, wallet and global limits. Wallet and global limits use rolling 24-hour windows. The owner-confirmed global ceiling on October 23 is 10,000,000 HTW across all players. A shared global allowance can prevent a request from someone who has not withdrawn personally.

Check your current review for per-request and wallet amounts, global allowance and any pause. Application limits can be lower than the immutable contract’s initialized numeric ceilings. Rolling limits do not reset at a fixed midnight. No exact reopening time is promised.

Saved requests retain their admitted terms. Observe and recover an uncertain original request. A timeout or one failed attempt does not prove all spending authority expired. A wallet signature or pending screen is not a finalized payout. Do not make a replacement payment to fix an uncertain original transaction.

## Funding and market risks

The historical reserve deposit and public source do not guarantee sustainability or returns. The backend remains trusted to assess activity and authorize credit. HTW and item values can fall and buyers can be unavailable. Game, wallet, RPC and signing-service outages can interrupt transactions. Read [reserve trust boundaries](reserve-and-transparency.md) and the [whitepaper](whitepaper.md).
