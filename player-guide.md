# Start your HEATWAVE fleet

Reviewed October 23, 2026 · [Documentation home](README.md)

You manage an automatic car fleet in Sundown Bay. Use the [live guide](https://heatwave.gg/codex-en.html#start) for current rules. A wallet and confirmed on-chain player registration are required. Paid random car boxes are offered; an eligible player-market car is another route.

## Connect, register and choose a car

Start with the wallet you intend to keep using:

1. Connect a supported Solana wallet and approve the sign-in message. This proves ownership and is separate from payment.
2. Review a paid box or eligible player-market car. Check its contents or exact item, restrictions, HTW price, SOL costs and any deposit.
3. Confirm registration. A new player’s first box purchase includes it in the same approval; an existing unregistered account activates once separately.
4. Follow the original saved request until confirmation and delivery. Recover an uncertain request instead of paying again.
5. Press **Start fleet** once your account and car are eligible.

Delivery adds a recorded game car to your collection. It is not a claimed NFT mint or guaranteed resale value.

## Manage active and recovering cars

Up to 15 cars work at once, with rarer ready cars taking the next free slot. Your collection can exceed 15. Waiting cars need an active slot; recovering cars need energy. Each departure uses 5% energy, giving 20 departures from a full battery. Outside recovery is free; garages and the mechanic are optional.

Pause prevents new departures while current trips finish. Recall is a separate interruption. Replacing an eligible active car uses a ready reserve while the replaced car rests to full energy. Waiting or recovering does not mean a car disappeared.

## Read activity and balances

Pending activity is recorded work awaiting assessment, not spendable HTW. Assessment runs after the relevant UTC day closes. Only the funded portion becomes confirmed credit; a partial or zero allocation creates no debt. Eligible recorded work in an open mission can enter assessment without completing the mission.

Available game credit, reserved withdrawal credit and external wallet HTW are separate. Hourly, daily and fiat references do not guarantee income or payment. Read [economy and fees](economy-and-fees.md) before withdrawing.

## Check the announced pass change

The Auto-Farm Pass costs 50,000 credited HTW plus 0.005 SOL for seven days. From October 24 at 12:00 UTC / 09:00 Brasília, a fleet without a pass starts new trips only while the game is open. Trips already underway finish; an open game works with or without a pass. Until that time, existing farming rules apply. The pass does not remove energy, offline-progress or funding limits.

## Get public help

Use the official Discord’s player-support or bug-reports channels. Share your browser, wallet provider, affected screen, exact error and approximate time, with private details hidden. Staff will never need your password, recovery phrase, private key or authentication code. Do not approve a transaction that differs from the review you intended.
