## Delivery and referrals

A paid order keeps its original quote and recorded contents. A timeout or delayed reveal is not a reason to pay again. Recover the original order with the same wallet. Opening a delivered box adds no reroll or token charge.

One linked friend qualifies one referral car box after at least 125,000 HTW in finalized paid box purchases. Link the invitation before the first box order. Each friend qualifies once. Self-referrals, pending payments, market purchases and free openings do not qualify. The delivered referral car is account-bound and cannot be sold. It is a game item, not an HTW payout.

Older paired orders retain their recorded character/car contents. They do not define today’s car-only offers. See the [whitepaper](whitepaper.md) for fusion and other HTW uses.
