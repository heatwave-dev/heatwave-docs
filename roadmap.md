# HEATWAVE roadmap

Updated October 23, 2026 · [Documentation home](README.md) · [Whitepaper](whitepaper.md)

This roadmap separates features you can use now, an announced scheduled rule and milestones that still need confirmation. Dates describe specific announced behavior. They do not guarantee credits, exchange acceptance or resolution of every player case.

## Available now

The current game includes:

| Area | Available behavior | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automatic fleet | Up to 15 active cars, ready-reserve rotation, energy and free outside recovery | [Farm and energy](https://heatwave.gg/codex-en.html#farm) |
| Collection and Showroom | Seven car rarities and a Showroom covering 60 models | [Cars](https://heatwave.gg/codex-en.html#cars) |
| Paid boxes and market | Random paid car boxes and eligible listed cars as an alternative | [Boxes](https://heatwave.gg/codex-en.html#boxes) |
| Optional facilities | Garages and the mechanic for recovery and readiness | [Garages](https://heatwave.gg/codex-en.html#garages) |
| Car Fusion | Eligible cars consumed for a random model of the next supported rarity | [Fusion costs](whitepaper.md#how-htw-is-used) |
| Auto-Farm Pass | Seven-day pass purchased with credited HTW plus SOL | [Pass rules](https://heatwave.gg/codex-en.html#farm) |
| Credits and withdrawals | Funded daily assessment, saved-request recovery and conditional HTW withdrawal | [Earnings](https://heatwave.gg/codex-en.html#gains) |

Wallet-first withdrawal compatibility was deployed on October 23. Real-wallet confirmation that the reported warning disappeared remains separate from deployment. This roadmap does not mark every affected player case as resolved.

## Scheduled: October 24, 2026 at 12:00 UTC

The announced Auto-Farm Pass departure rule starts at **12:00 UTC / 09:00 Brasília**:

- Without an active pass, new trips start only while the game is open.
- Trips already underway finish under their recorded terms.
- With the game open, the fleet works with or without a pass.
- Energy, eligible away-progress and funding limits still apply.

Until that time, existing farming behavior applies. The pass does not promise a fixed credit or withdrawal amount.
